# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and to any facilitated session
run using this material.

## Our commitment

This project exists so that children — all of them, and girls specifically, who are
underrepresented in STEM — can find out that they belong in technical work. That
purpose is incompatible with an environment where people are sorted by who they are.

## Expected

- Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith until shown otherwise.
- Critique the change, not the person.
- When someone reports that something in the material made them or a learner feel
  excluded, take it as information about the material. It is not a verdict on you.
- Respect that contributors and users include minors. Do not ask for, collect, or
  publish a learner's personal information.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Demeaning comments about gender, race, disability, sexuality, age, religion,
  nationality, or appearance — including "just a joke" versions and including
  claims about who is naturally suited to technical work.
- Sexualised attention or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Dismissing an accessibility report because "nobody uses that".

## Reporting

Report to the maintainer, [@myzticalbabe](https://github.com/myzticalbabe), by
opening a private report through the repository's **Security → Report a
vulnerability** form, or by direct message. Reports about conduct are handled
confidentially.

## Enforcement

| Severity | Response |
|---|---|
| Correctable misunderstanding | A private note naming the behaviour and the expected one |
| Repeated or dismissive behaviour | Comment removed, warning, temporary interaction limit |
| Harassment or a slur | Permanent ban from the repository's spaces |
| Anything involving a minor's safety | Immediate ban and report to the platform |

Enforcement decisions are made by the maintainer and are not up for debate in
public threads. Appeals go to the same private channel.
